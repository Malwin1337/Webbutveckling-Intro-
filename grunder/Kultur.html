Fika

The english word for Fika dosen't have an direct translation, the closet we can come to is "Coffe break"

Coffe break is often a socially interaction with people, and a tradition. The most important thing of it is having a break from work and be social around people, the food is either coffe or tea with a sweet treat as a side

It is an very important thing for the swedish people.
