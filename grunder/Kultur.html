Fika

The english word for Fika dosen't have an direct translation, the closet we can come to is "Coffe break"

Coffe break is often a socially interaction with people, and a tradition. The most important thing of it is having a break from work and be social around people, the food is either coffe or tea with a sweet treat as a side

It is an very important thing for the swedish people, and is mandatory.

Midsummer

Midsummer(Midsommar) is one of Swedens most important holidays, celebrated annually on Friday between June 19 to June 25 to welcome summer and the longest day of the year.

Some core traditions in Midsummer is the Maypole (Midsommar stång) where people run around, hold hand togheter and dance. People wears flower crowns and so on.

Midsummer is also famous for Magic and Folklore.

Crayfish party

Crayfish parry (Kräftskiva) is a festiv August celebration centerd around eating cold, dill-boiled Crayfish peeled by hand. The meal is traditionally served with Västerbotten cheese quiche, crispbread, and strong cheese. The party is famous for its lively, informal atmosphere, featuring colorful paper lanterns, comical hats, and snaps paired with traditional drinking songs before every toast.
